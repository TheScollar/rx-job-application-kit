# Target roles & criteria

> What I am open to and what I am filtering out. Update when priorities
> shift. /apply pre-screens every JD against this file.

## Role titles I'd consider

- Senior Product Manager (platform, data, or analytics scope)
- Lead Product Manager
- Group Product Manager (if the data domain is core)

## Stage / company profile

- Scale-up (Series B+) with real revenue: preferred
- Bootstrapped and profitable: yes
- Early startup (Seed/A): only with a strong founding team and data focus
- Corporate/enterprise: no

## Domain preferences

**Strong yes:**
- B2B SaaS analytics / data platforms
- Developer- or API-adjacent products
- E-commerce infrastructure

**Open:**
- Fintech (payments)
- Productivity tooling

**Filter out:**
- Crypto / gambling
- Ad-tech

## Location & format

- Primary location: Berlin
- Onsite/hybrid/remote: hybrid up to 2 days onsite, or remote (Germany)
- Relocation: no

## Compensation band

- Base salary: 95.000 EUR floor
- Variable / OTE: open
- Equity: nice to have
- Notice period: 2 months

## Dealbreakers

- No product strategy ("engineering decides")
- Analytics is a bolt-on, not the product
- Hire-and-fire culture
- Travel more than 1 day/week

## Must-haves

- Clear product mandate (strategy + execution)
- Real data infrastructure and appetite to invest in it
- Engineering that respects PM craft
- Sustainable pace
